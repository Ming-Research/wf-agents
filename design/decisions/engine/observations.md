# The observations

What agents come across beyond their works, kept for the owner as the installation's observations.

### decision observations-kept-with-the-finish
A run keeps, as it finishes, the observations of the return it was taken on and of each return of it sent back that no later return of it was made in a session holding; they are kept with the rest of the work and numbered in the same transaction. Their text, their cell and when they were kept are read from the work's records and are not copied.
Why: A later return made in the session that held one sent back says again what of it still holds, so keeping both would keep it twice; but a session begun anew after it (for the context's limit, another model or account, the owner, a flag) or begun again or compacted under its id holds only why it was sent back, and keeping only the return taken lost what the earlier one said. Saved with the finish, each is kept once whatever stops between (principle effects-known-as-done); the records hold the text already (principles state-from-records and stored-content-is-kept-small).

### decision observations-read-beside-the-engines-turn
The observations are read for the page, and the owner's reading of one is kept, through connections of their own to the store's file, not through the engine's one turn of changes to the works' records.
Why: Nothing decides by them and the tables of the readings are only added to, so a second writer cannot make a work's records diverge, as with the backup's records (decision backup-records-are-written-beside-the-engines-turn).
