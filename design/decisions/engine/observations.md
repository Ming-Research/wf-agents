# The observations

What agents come across beyond their works, kept for the owner as the installation's observations.

### decision observations-kept-with-the-finish
Each text of `observations` in the return a run was taken on is kept as an observation, and numbered, as the run's finish is saved and in the same transaction; a return sent back keeps none. Its text, its cell and when it was kept are read from the work's records and are not copied.
Why: Saved with the finish, an observation is kept once whatever stops between (principle effects-known-as-done); the agent says again in its next return what one sent back said, so keeping those would keep it twice. The records hold the text already (principles state-from-records and stored-content-is-kept-small).

### decision observations-read-beside-the-engines-turn
The observations are read for the page, and the owner's reading of one is kept, through connections of their own to the store's file, not through the engine's one turn of changes to the works' records.
Why: Nothing decides by them and their tables are only added to, so a second writer cannot make a work's records diverge, as with the backup's records (decision backup-records-are-written-beside-the-engines-turn).
