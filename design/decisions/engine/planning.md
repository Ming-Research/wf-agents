# Planning

How the items are made and changed, and their works let on, by whoever plans the work.

### decision no-flow-of-the-engine-plans-the-work
Items are made, changed and let on through the program's commands, by the owner, the assistant and the architect's session outside the engine; no flow of the engine plans the work.
- replaced: An architect's flow that drove the project by its policy, making and ordering the items, was on main; the owner changed it, because where a project goes mostly needs his word, the assistant having answered 27 of the engineers' 35 questions for him.

### decision a-work-begins-only-when-let-on
An item's work is added paused, and begins nothing until whoever plans the work lets it on.
- replaced: A work beginning by itself as its item was added was on main; the owner changed it, because that was a defect: works are let on by hand, so that whoever plans can put something that comes up first.

### decision help-says-everything-in-one-call
`wf-agents help` gives, in one call, every command's usage, arguments and options, those of the commands under it too.
- replaced: A list of the commands, each needing its own `--help`, was on main; the owner changed it, because one call should say everything.
