# Planning

How whoever plans the work makes and changes the items and lets their works run.

### decision no-flow-of-the-engine-plans-the-work
The owner, the assistant and the architect's session outside the engine make and change items, and let their works run, through the program's commands; no flow of the engine plans the work.
- replaced: An architect's flow that drove the project by its policy, making and ordering the items, was on main; the owner changed it, because where a project goes mostly needs his word, and the assistant had answered 27 of the engineers' 35 questions for him.

### decision a-work-begins-only-when-let-on
An item's work is added paused, and starts nothing until whoever plans the work lets it run.
- replaced: A work starting by itself when its item was added was on main; the owner changed it, because that was a defect: works are started by hand, so that whoever plans can put first something that comes up.

### decision help-says-everything-in-one-call
`wf-agents help` gives, in one call, the usage, arguments and options of every command, and of the commands under each.
- replaced: A list of the commands, each needing its own `--help`, was on main; the owner changed it, because one call should say everything.
