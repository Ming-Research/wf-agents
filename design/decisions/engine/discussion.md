# What is said to a work

How the owner's words reach the cell the engine chooses, and how what is said is kept (design document
`data-and-context`, 30 to 38).

### decision words-go-only-to-a-cell-that-can-take-them
The owner's words go only to a cell they run whose condition is met now, the engine choosing it and the page showing its choice before and after the words are sent; where there is none, or the work has ended, they are refused with why and go to no other cell. A line none of whose cells the owner's words run is not counted as standing anywhere.
Why: The page chose the cell itself and, finding none for the cell that asked, wrote the words for the cell before it without a word (work 4); words written for a cell that does not apply would wait unseen (principle nothing-passes-for-normal). Counted as where the line stands, the reading of the tree a reset or compacted session begins with, which no words run, left the line taking none until a later cell ran again.

### decision an-entry-keeps-its-cell-apart-from-its-words
Of each entry of the record of what is said, the cell the owner's words were given to is kept with their write, and the cell that answered is the one whose run wrote it; the content says who spoke in the words its file's `owner` and `reply` give, which name no cell. The page reads the cells from the writes, and takes only the words out of the content by those texts.
Why: A cell is given the whole record as its content stands, the entries to the other cells of its line among them, a round is not told who wrote a version (design document `data-and-context`, 29), and a cell's words name no cell (principle an-agent-knows-only-its-task); a cell read back out of the words, as was done, made the page parse what is data. Saying the writer in the words that give every datum of kind `all`, as was tried, was turned down: it changed how every datum is given for the need of one; and reading the cell from the block of the owner's words the same act wrote, as was tried, named no cell for an entry that act did not write.

### decision a-record-of-what-is-said-without-its-texts-is-refused-as-the-flow-loads
A record of what is said (a block the engine's source of the owner's words writes entries of, which a trigger of it has its agent read) is refused by the flow's compile where its `writes` give no `owner` or no `reply`, or give one without its place, `{words}` in the owner's and `{reply}` in the cell's, each fault said against the record and the text it lacks, with every other fault of the flow. The compiled flow carries the two texts of such a record whole, and the engine reads them from it, never as a default.
Why: Taken as empty where missing, the engine kept the owner's or an agent's non-empty words as an empty entry without a word (principle nothing-passes-for-normal, decision a-write-of-nothing-only-empties); found as the flow loads, it is mended before any round runs, and the engine has no case of a text that is not there to handle. Checked only by the driver as it starts, as the keys were, the place was never looked for, and a flow that was compiled but not driven passed.

### decision words-to-a-round-count-once-its-session-held-them
Words told to a round under way count as taken by it only once its program has ended, and only those its session is found to have been given; the rest stay new to the cell, which runs again for them. So too for a run abandoned meanwhile: its return is dropped, but what its session was given counts.
Why: Counted as taken when said, words reached a round whose agent used no tool again and were consumed unseen, and its session was given them again with the next message, since nothing said it held them; the same where the round was then stopped.
