# Questions and answers

What the engine keeps of the questions put to the owner and of the owner's answers, and what it writes of them.

### decision the-merge-gate-is-a-datum-the-engine-writes
Whether every question of the work that stands has the owner's answer to its latest version is the datum `asked`, a
choice the engine writes as a question is asked, asked anew or withdrawn and as the owner answers, where its value or
its words would change; before any question it is `answered` by its default. A cell waits for it as for any datum, by
its precondition: the check before the merge requires `answered`.
Why: A wait of the walk's own for the merge, which a cell would have named by a key of its own, was turned down: it
would be a kind of wait the flow's conditions cannot say and the page cannot show as it shows every precondition
(Article 7). Written only where it changes, it runs no cell.

### decision an-answer-runs-its-cell-by-the-engines-source
The block of the trigger an answer runs is written by the engine's source `answer` as the owner answers, never by the
owner nor by anything outside the work: the owner's act is the answer, and the engine works out from the question
whether it runs the cell. Neither that block nor `asked` is taken as written from outside.
Why: A block the owner wrote would run the cell for an answer that takes the option the work goes by, which statement
35 of the design document `data-and-context` says runs nothing, and would not stop a later round under way, as the
owner turning to a cell does; and a write of either from outside would say of the questions what their records do not.

### decision an-answer-to-an-earlier-version-is-kept
An answer to a version of a question that a later version replaced is kept in the owner's record, and answers nothing:
it runs no cell and leaves `asked` as it was. It is not refused.
Why: The owner answered what the page showed, which a round may have asked anew in the meantime; the record keeps what
the owner did (principle the-past-as-it-ran), and the question asked anew waits for an answer of its own.

### decision protected-paths-are-looked-at-as-each-round-ends
The engine looks at what the work's branch changes under the paths the project protects, against where it left main,
as each round of a line that may change the work ends with a return, and asks the owner by itself, as a question of
that round's cell that waits: once for each content of the change, again under its name where the change is another,
and withdrawn once the work touches none of the paths. A look that fails asks nothing, and the next round's look finds
what it missed.
Why: Asked at the check before the merge, the owner's "take it back" reached no cell that could take the change back;
asked of the round that made it, the answer runs that cell by its trigger of an answer. Asking at every round that
touches the paths was turned down: the owner would answer the same change again and again.

### decision an-answer-is-an-entry-of-what-its-cell-reads
The owner's answer to a question's latest version is written, in the words of the datum's own file, as the owner's
entry of each datum the asking cell's trigger of an answer has its agent read, `answers` in the engineer's flow, as well
as kept in the record of answers the walk reads; an answer to an earlier version is no such entry.
Why: A cell is given what it reads as any datum, by the kind its file names, and so a session reopened holds every
answer; a second way of giving the answer, as the result of a call once was, was turned down (design document
`data-and-context`).
