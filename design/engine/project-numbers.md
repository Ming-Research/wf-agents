Decision: The engine measures how many of a project's works run together from the rounds of the engineer's works on the project's items, counting a work as running only while it has a round under way that is not a wait for an answer (the cells that ask the owner or the architect, whose round stays open for the whole wait, and under that cell's ids in every flow version), apart from the architect's works, instead of every round under way, because the round of a work waiting for the owner or the architect stays open for as long as the answer takes, so that counting all rounds reports works as running together when none ran, which is the opposite of what the policy's signal about parallel running needs to show. A round of a tool cell counts as running for now, provisionally: a work waiting for CI holds its slot, and the count can be reopened if the owner wants it apart.

Decision: The engine counts, for each week, the engineer's works of the project that ended without their item being merged by this work, each once and by the kind of end (the flow moving to a new version, the item cancelled, the work started over, the end of the graph reached without a merge, other), instead of counting items, or grouping by the text the works table records, because the work is what the signal compares with merges, one item can lose several works, and the text carries a version number and a clause that differ for every flow move, so it names a kind only by its start. A work with a merged round never counts, even when its item was cancelled after it, because it ended by its merge.

Decision: A week in which nothing ran or ended shows zero for each of these numbers, not a missing value, and the weeks are those the project's other numbers use, instead of a week of its own, because the policy's signals compare these numbers week by week with the others.

Rejected:
- The history of which works run, kept in a table of its own: rejected because it needs a migration and a write at every place a work starts, waits, pauses or ends, and weeks before it would be empty, while the rounds and the changes already say what these two numbers need.
- Rebuilding each work's running stretches from the changes to its cell and its pause: rejected because it replays every change of every work to reach what the cell of an open round already tells, and an older flow version's cell would have to be read by a second rule.
