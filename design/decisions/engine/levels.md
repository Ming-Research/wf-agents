# The levels

What an item's level chooses for the rounds of its agents, the reasoning effort each cell runs at, and which lines run above their item's level.

### decision a-level-is-a-difficulty
An item has one level, which says how hard its work is and chooses only the model its agents run on, never their effort; a level's description speaks of difficulty alone.
- replaced: Five levels, each a model and an effort together, stood on main; the owner changed it, because a step should think as hard as it needs, a gate little whatever the item, which one effort for all of a level's steps could not say.
- rejected: an agent proposed telling the levels apart by whether a work needs new decisions; the owner rejected it, because a level is how hard the architect understands the work to be, which has nothing to do with whether it needs the decision tree.
- replaced: A level's description that raised an item where its work met new decisions or facts to find stood on main; the owner changed it, because items were rated too high, this project's work needing no third level.
- replaced: One model for every round, the installation's single setting, stood on main; the owner changed it, because small items then ran on Opus, where the old system had most work at level 2.

### decision effort-is-the-flows
Every agent cell names its reasoning effort in the flow's definition, none of them the lowest, and neither the page nor the installation's settings has a setting of it.
- rejected: an agent proposed keeping each cell's effort in the installation's settings, changed on the page as the owner had asked; the owner rejected it, because should one need changing, a change of the flow then is no trouble.
- replaced: Searching and judging cells on the cheapest model at the lowest effort stood on main; the owner changed it, because the cheapest model at the lowest effort judges too poorly.

### decision the-research-and-the-code-reviews-think-high
The initial research and the reviews of the change run at the effort high, the implementation, the deep research and the plan's reviews at xhigh.
- replaced: The initial research and the reviews of the change at xhigh stood on main; the owner changed it, because high might take a third less of a research that took 7 to 13 minutes, though that was not measured.

### decision the-deep-research-and-the-plan-review-run-above
The deep research and the plan's reviews run one level above their item's, by their lines' `levels_above`, the code's reviews at its own, and nothing above the installation's highest for items.
- replaced: Items at any level, the top one included, stood on main; the owner changed it, because the top level's model is not much stronger than the one below it and costs far more.
- rejected: an agent proposed running the review a level higher where the design tree changes; the owner rejected it, because the item's own level is enough until a trial of both shows that a level more helps.
- replaced: The deep research at its item's level stood on main; the owner changed it, because a weaker agent may fix the surface and stop, hiding a larger problem that no later step finds, and with few works going that deep the higher model costs little.
- replaced: The deep research raised one level even past the highest level an item may be at, onto the top level, stood on main; the owner changed it, because the highest level is the most for the whole installation.
- replaced: The plan review at its item's level stood on main; the owner changed it, because engineers mostly do what a review says, so a review weaker than the plan it reviews turns good work bad, which he held to again once it ran on every work.
