# What is said to a work

How the owner's words reach the cell the engine chooses, and how what is said is kept (design document
`data-and-context`, 30 to 39).

### decision words-go-only-to-a-cell-that-can-take-them
A cell can take the owner's words only where the owner's words run it and its condition is met now. Where the cell
the engine chooses cannot, where no cell of the line that the owner's words run has run and the work stands at none of
them, or where the work has ended, the words are refused with why, and go to no other cell. A cell of the line the
owner's words do not run is not where the line stands. The work's page carries the engine's choice for each line and
for each question that stands, so that the page shows the cell, or why there is none, before anything is sent, and
the act that says them answers with the cell the engine gave them to, which the page then names.
Why: The page chose the cell itself and, finding none for R2, wrote the words for R1 without a word (work 4). Words
written for a cell that does not apply would wait unseen, as it would never run for them (principle
nothing-passes-for-normal). Counted as where the line stands, the reading of the tree a reset or compacted session
begins with, which no words run, left the engineer's line taking none until the implementation ran again.

### decision an-entry-says-in-its-words-who-spoke-to-whom
Each entry of the record of what is said holds in its content who spoke and to which cell, in the words its file's
`owner` and `reply` give; the page takes the cell and the words back out of an entry by those words.
Why: A cell is given the whole record, the entries to the other cells of its line among them, and a round is not told
who wrote a version (design document `data-and-context`, 29). Saying the writer in the words that give every datum of
kind `all`, as was tried, was turned down: it changed how every datum is given for the need of one. Reading the cell
from the block of the owner's words the same act wrote, as was tried, named no cell for an entry carried from before
the record.

### decision words-to-a-round-count-once-its-hook-gave-them
Words told to a round under way are put into it only as its program has ended, of those its carrier finds that the
hook gave the session; what still waited for the hook is taken back, and stays new to the cell, which runs again for
it. Of a run abandoned meanwhile, what the hook gave is put in all the same, until its round has ended: its return is
dropped, and the words stay new to the cell, but its session holds them.
Why: Put in as they were said, a round whose agent used no tool again consumed words it never saw, and the session
the hook gave them to was given them again with the next message, since nothing said it held them; left out of a round
that was then stopped, they were given again so too.

### decision words-kept-before-the-record-are-carried-into-it
The owner's words a work kept before the record of what is said held them, in a cell's block of the owner's words as a
person wrote it, are carried into the record as the driver opens the store: the owner's entry to that cell, once,
after everything the work holds; they run no cell.
Why: That block is the engine's alone now and has no words: the words were in neither the record nor the page, and no
session that reopened was given them. Run again for them, the cell would answer what it may have answered.
