# What rounds cost

How the engine judges what the rounds, the calls of tools and the works take, and tells a round that missed its cache.

### decision a-line-comes-from-the-history-of-its-kind
Every line a round, an act or a call of a tool is judged by is worked out from the history of its kind on the flow; with too few of them, nothing is judged.
- rejected: an agent proposed fixed limits on a round, a work and a day, and a session's bound lowered by hand; the owner rejected it, because a number set by hand that pins one point is not a good way.
- rejected: an agent proposed three fixed limits on what is sent to an agent, on one input, on a round's own content and on a whole message; the owner rejected it, because a fixed threshold misses small faults and flags large sends that are sound.

### decision the-guard-stops-on-growth-alone
The run guard stops a round only where its context grows, or its first prompt jumps, past two lines at once; what a round costs and how long it takes stop nothing.
- rejected: an agent proposed cutting a long round at a line and handing its session over to a new one in the same cell; the owner rejected it, because how an agent uses its own tools is not the system's efficiency, which is what is to be mended.

### decision a-letting-on-allows-what-the-installation-says
A work may spend its budget, and `agents.let_on_allowance_usd` more each time the owner let it on after the engine paused it for its spend; the words beside it say how much more.
- replaced: Letting on a work paused for its budget allowing nothing more was on main; the owner changed it, because one press then bought a single round, and the work was paused again at its end.
- replaced: Words beside a work paused for its budget that said only what it had spent over its budget was on main; the owner changed it, because they are to say how much a letting-on adds.

### decision a-miss-is-told-from-the-records
A round missed its session's cache where, within the cache's life, its first call read under half of what the session held: expected where what precedes the messages changed, else wf-agents' fault.
- rejected: an agent proposed counting a miss into the cost figures a project's architect reads; the owner rejected it, because the project run need not be wf-agents, and a miss is wf-agents' to mend, so it is to come to him.
