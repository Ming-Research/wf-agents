# What is said to a work

How the owner's words reach a cell of a work, and how the cell answers them.

### decision each-cell-that-asks-has-a-dialogue-with-the-owner
Each cell that may ask has a datum for the owner's words, `{cell}_words`, which the owner and the cell write and only that cell watches; every agent cell is given all the words of every cell.
- replaced: One record of everything said in the work, `discussion`, with a block made to wake each cell spoken to, was on main; the owner changed it, because only the cell spoken to watches its own words, so they wake only that cell and no block is made to wake it.
- rejected: an agent proposed giving each cell only the owner's words said to it; the owner rejected it, because guidance he gives once is guidance everyone needs to know.
- rejected: an agent proposed writing in the text of each entry whom the words were said to, for the page to read back; the owner rejected it, because a page picking names out of a text is a fault in the data's design.
- rejected: an agent proposed one datum for both a cell's questions and the owner's words to it; the owner rejected it, because a question is a question, carries no words, and is answered only by an option.
- rejected: an agent proposed that the owner's words not be any cell's datum, but be said to a line and answered in a round that only answers; the owner rejected it, because the engine has no way to send to a line, and words that are not a datum leave unsaid how the cells run on them.

### decision a-flow-is-refused-where-a-cell-that-asks-takes-no-words
A flow is refused as it loads if a cell in it writes questions of its own but watches no words of its own.
- replaced: Letting cells ask with nothing to take the owner's words was on main; the owner changed it, because the words he says about a question would then go to another cell, which does not know why it was asked.

### decision words-go-only-to-a-cell-that-can-take-them
The owner's words go to a cell, or to a line and so to the cell the line has reached, as the engine chooses and the page shows; if that cell cannot take them, they are refused with the reason.
- replaced: The page choosing the cell itself, and sending words to the initial research when the cell that asked could not take them, was on main; the owner changed it, because words go to the cell the line has reached, and words on a question go to the cell that asked it.
- rejected: an agent proposed leaving out of this decision that the page shows the engine's choice of cell before and after the words are sent; the owner rejected it, because the page choosing for itself was what had gone wrong.

### decision words-are-put-into-a-round-under-way
Words to a cell whose round is under way are put into its session by a hook, right after its next use of one of its program's tools; words that did not reach the round run the cell again.
- rejected: an agent proposed that the words wait for the round to end, and the cell then run again for them; the owner rejected it, because when the owner speaks, he usually wants the work under way to change course now.

### decision a-cell-spoken-to-answers-at-once
A cell spoken to hands in its answer before doing any more of its work: its words say so, and words put into a round under way end by telling it to stop where it is and hand in its answer now.
- replaced: Handing in the answer with the round's return at its end, with the words put in saying only to answer in the cell's words, was on main; the owner changed it, because the cell should stop, answer him first, and then go back to its work.
- rejected: an agent proposed a short answer first, with the work going on in the same round; the owner rejected it, because the cell should stop and answer before anything else.
- replaced: Putting in words that ended by telling the cell to mark its words as dealt with once it had dealt with them was on main; the owner changed it, because the answer should come at once, and a fork read those words as leave to wait.
- rejected: an agent proposed a longer sentence that also said to leave the words out of what is dealt with until the work is done, with two other changes; the owner rejected it, because each fix added words, when only what caused the fault should change.

### decision the-owners-words-are-never-withdrawn
Words the owner has sent are never withdrawn: no version of a cell's words is taken back or marked withdrawn, before the cell reads it or after.
- rejected: an agent proposed letting words to an agent be withdrawn before it had them, marked withdrawn and never deleted; the owner rejected it, because he never withdrew anything and needs no such thing.
