# The engine

What holds wherever the engine puts together the words it gives an agent: in a line's system prompt, in a cell's
message and in what it tells a round under way.

### decision a-text-not-the-flows-words-is-fenced
Where the engine fills a place of the flow's words with a whole text that is not the flow's, as the content of a datum or what changed in it, a call's result, what the owner said to a round under way, or a file of the project in the system prompt, it gives it in a fenced block of its own, the fence longer than any run of backticks in the text and the text as it is between; a value that stands within a sentence, as a name, a number, a title or a choice's value, is given as it is.
Why: Given bare, what such a text held became the structure of what held it (principle agent-writing-is-data): a decision a plan was to add, its first line `# Greeting in the evening`, stood among a review's own headings as the one every later part fell under, and the end of a project's `design/README.md` could not be told from the rules after it. Putting the text's headings below the message's was turned down: it changes the text, which an agent may hand in again or write into a file, and misses a heading made by a line of `=` or `-` under a line of text; tags around the text were turned down, since the text may hold the tag that closes them. That an agent follows a file of the project given in a fence as it follows one given bare rests on judgment.

### decision a-sentence-ends-once-where-a-place-ends-it
Where the flow's words put a full stop right after a place and what fills the place ends a sentence already, in English or in Chinese (`.`, `?`, `!`, `。`, `？`, `！`), the stop is left out, in every filling of the engine's words; a stop that a word follows at once, as a file's extension, stays.
Why: The record of the questions and the answers read `边界。.` where what followed from the owner's option ended in a Chinese full stop and its words put a stop after the place. Taking the stop out of the words and making what fills the place a sentence, as a reason is made one, was turned down here: what follows from an option is written by the agent that asks, the words read as a sentence only with their stop, and only the joining sees both sides of the place.
