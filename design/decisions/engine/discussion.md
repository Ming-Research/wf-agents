# What is said to a work

How the owner's words reach the cell the engine chooses, and how what is said is kept (design document
`data-and-context`, 30 to 38).

### decision words-go-only-to-a-cell-that-can-take-them
The owner's words go only to a cell they run whose condition is met now, the engine choosing it and the page showing its choice before and after the words are sent; where there is none, or the work has ended, they are refused with why and go to no other cell. A line none of whose cells the owner's words run is not counted as standing anywhere.
Why: The page chose the cell itself and, finding none for the cell that asked, wrote the words for the cell before it without a word (work 4); words written for a cell that does not apply would wait unseen (principle nothing-passes-for-normal). Counted as where the line stands, the reading of the tree a reset or compacted session begins with, which no words run, left the line taking none until a later cell ran again.

### decision an-entry-says-in-its-words-who-spoke-to-whom
Each entry of the record of what is said holds in its content who spoke and to which cell, in the words its file's `owner` and `reply` give; the page takes the cell and the words back out of an entry by those words.
Why: A cell is given the whole record, the entries to the other cells of its line among them, and a round is not told who wrote a version (design document `data-and-context`, 29). Saying the writer in the words that give every datum of kind `all`, as was tried, was turned down: it changed how every datum is given for the need of one. Reading the cell from the block of the owner's words the same act wrote, as was tried, named no cell for an entry that act did not write.

### decision words-to-a-round-count-once-its-session-held-them
Words told to a round under way count as taken by it only once its program has ended, and only those its session is found to have been given; the rest stay new to the cell, which runs again for them. So too for a run abandoned meanwhile: its return is dropped, but what its session was given counts.
Why: Counted as taken when said, words reached a round whose agent used no tool again and were consumed unseen, and its session was given them again with the next message, since nothing said it held them; the same where the round was then stopped.
