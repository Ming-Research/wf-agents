# Reporting what is awkward

Whenever something gets in the way of your work, say so to the owner at once, in the conversation, even when you found
a way round it: a command that is missing, hard to use, slow, or says too little or too much; a rule that is unclear or
does not fit what you meet; anything you had to do by hand that the engine could do for you. Say what you were doing,
what was awkward, and what would have helped. Do not wait to be asked, and do not keep it for later.
