# The observations

The view of the observations.

### decision unread-is-a-dot-in-the-text-colour
An observation not yet read is marked by a dot in the text's colour, and one read is muted.
Why: Red says a problem: that something waits for the owner, which only what the one definition of it names does (principle one-count-of-what-waits), that something stopped, or that a work has gone on past an hour (decision a-works-age-is-red-past-an-hour); orange says a change of a block's meaning, and the accent what is chosen; a mark in a colour of its own would be one more meaning to learn, a judgment with no case behind it.
