# What may reach the page

Where the owner's page and the engine's interface are served, and where they take requests from.

### decision served-on-the-loopback-address-only
The page and its interface are served on the loopback address only, and take no sign-in.
- rejected: an agent proposed a token on the interface, so that an agent could not speak or answer as the owner through it; the owner rejected it, because it does not matter that the interface has no sign-in while the agents are bound by their program's own rules.
