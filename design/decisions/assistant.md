# The owner's assistant

The session beside the owner that thinks designs through with him, and looks at the system and acts on it for him.

### decision the-assistant-works-outside-the-engine
The owner's assistant is a session outside the engine, which looks at the system and acts on it through the program's commands; nothing of it runs in the engine.
- rejected: an agent proposed running the assistant inside the system, its talk kept in the database; the owner rejected it, because inside it would die with the system, grow into a second architect with a queue, and hold the highest rights while running unattended.

### decision the-assistant-has-no-switch
Nothing in the system bounds what the assistant does for the owner: it works with his permissions, and no switch says how far it may confirm in his stead.
- rejected: an agent proposed a mechanical switch of how far the assistant may confirm in the owner's stead; the owner rejected it, because the assistant has full permission and does what he tells it.
