Decision: The owner starts and stops the works in two ways: every work at once, with "Pause all" and "Start all" in the page's bar, and each work, with its Pause, Continue and Step in its row of the works list and in its window. A work goes on by itself only while the works are not paused as a whole and the work is not paused itself; Pause all and a work's Pause take effect at the end of the round under way, which is never cut, because the engine's loop takes one step of one work at a time and reads between two steps what the owner asked; Start all lets go on every work the owner paused and lets new works start on ready items as slots free, while a work the engine paused for a problem stays paused, its reason shown, until the owner continues it on its own. Step runs exactly one round of a work that does not go on by itself, whether every work is paused, the owner paused it or the engine did, and the work stays as it was after the round; a work the engine paused that the owner steps is in the owner's hands from then on, its cell counting afresh as after any time the owner lets a work go on. Instead of two modes of the engine as a whole, running and holding, in a panel behind the engine's state, and one work's round only while the engine held, because the owner did not find the panel, wants every work paused without ending any (as before a new flow version is installed), and wants each work paused, let go on or stepped on its own.

Decision: Every work is paused as a whole whenever `wf-agents run` starts, whatever the owner had asked before it stopped, and nothing runs until the owner presses Start all or steps a work; what each work's own pause is (the owner's, or the engine's with its reason) is kept in the database and survives a restart, so that Start all after a restart goes on as it would have before. Instead of a command-line mode that starts every work, or the switch of every work kept across a restart, because the owner asked that the engine come up paused and be started from the page.

Decision: A work's state on the page keeps apart what holds it back though it could go on (paused by the owner, by its Pause or by Pause all; stopped by the engine, with the reason), which Continue or Start all lifts, and what it waits for, after which it goes on by itself (the owner's answer to an ask, the owner's ruling on a design card, the items it depends on), which Continue does nothing for and for which Step is not offered; both can hold at once and both are shown, and what a work waits for is read from what is open, never from the kind of cell it is at, so a work whose ask was answered is ready and says that the answer is in. The works list's design cards and asks to answer are the owner's to do, and a design card that does not hold the work up says on hovering that it is needed before the merge. Instead of one word, "paused", for both, and "asking" or "waiting" by the cell a work is at, because the owner asked why one work waiting for a ruling said waiting and another said asking, and a work held back and a work waiting call for different things of the owner.

Decision: Before the owner steps a work, its window shows what the round will be (the cell; an agent's round, a step of the engine, a run of tools or an ask of the owner; and the message the agent will be sent, built as the round would build it with nothing run or recorded) and says beside the button that it runs one real round on the owner's account, and Step pressed in the works list asks first, naming the work and the cell; while a round runs, the page shows how long it has run and what the agent is doing, as its command line reports it, and when the round the owner asked for ends, the page opens it, instead of a button with no word of what it runs, because one round is run to look at what it was given and what it returned, and both are known before and after.

Rejected:
- `run --once`, which takes a whole pass: rejected because a pass can run several rounds of several works.
- A single-step mode for the engine as a whole: rejected because which work its step took could not be told before it ran, and its control was everywhere.
- Continue of one work going on while every work is paused: rejected because the owner's words were that a paused engine starts nothing until Start all; Step runs one round of one work then.

Decision: A work's next round that the engine would not run, because its judgment takes a row that waits (the merge gate waiting for the owner's ruling on a design card, or for an item depended on to merge), or an ask waiting for the owner's answer, is shown as what the work waits for, with a link to the design card to rule on or the ask to answer, and no Step; a work that waits is asked again, so that once what it waits for came its next round is offered, instead of offering a round that records nothing new, or none at all while every work is paused, because the owner then does what the work waits for, and a pause must not leave a work no one can take on.

Decision: Building a work's next message to look at it (`wf-agents message`, and the page's preview inside the running engine) writes nothing: the command makes the engine only to read, the database opened read-only and as it is, so no flow version is recorded and no work stopped or started, instead of making the engine as it runs, because the command runs beside an engine that may be running, and a look must not change what it looks at.
