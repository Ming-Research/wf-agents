# The system

The view of how the whole system stands, and the settings of the installation.

### decision the-copies-stand-on-the-system-view
How the copies of the database stand is said in the System view's part of what goes on now, and nowhere on the Works page.
- replaced: A line at the head of the Works page, grey, red or orange, was on main; the owner changed it, because the backup is the installation's and not a work's, and the line told nothing while all was well.

### decision the-shared-wait-is-said-without-the-trace
The wait every work shares is said as what is down since when, what the engine's check found, and what it holds, never the round that met it, its error, or when the engine checks next.
- rejected: an agent proposed six sentences, the round that met it and the command line's own words among them; the owner rejected it, because the last two and the words that it was not signed in said one thing three times.
- replaced: One last sentence for every cause, that no round begins until the command line runs again, was on main; the owner changed it, because it did not read for a limit, the network or the service, so each cause says what ends it.

### decision the-page-changes-what-is-tuned-while-the-engine-runs
The Settings view changes the owner's language, each level's model, the highest level an item may be at, the context past which a line's session begins anew, and the slots; other keys only the file.
- replaced: Four settings of the installation's file shown, and none changed on the page, was on main; the owner changed it, because the page showed too little, and what is tuned as works go on should be changed there.
- replaced: The levels' models set in the installation's file alone was on main; the owner changed it, because he wanted the models of the levels, and what goes with them, in the page's settings.

### decision the-accounts-are-used-removed-and-added-there
Beside them the Settings view lists the accounts, the one in use marked and each other with Use, taken at once, and Remove, asked once; an account is added by its name and its token.
- replaced: A table of the program's directories, one an account, signed in by hand and taken once the owner said yes, was on main; the owner changed it, because a switch no longer loses the sessions, so one press may switch.

### decision the-usage-column-names-the-services-windows
An account's Usage, its table's second column, says on one line each window the service names, how much of it is used, and how long until it resets and when, counted down while the view is open.
- replaced: The columns Account, Added, Token lasts until and Usage, a reset given as a date, was on main; the owner changed it, because the day added and the token's end were of no use, and he wanted how long until each reset.
- replaced: A usage limit shown only for an account a round had met it under was on main; the owner changed it, because when switching he most wants to know which account has the most left.
