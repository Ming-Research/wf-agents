Decision work-controls: The owner starts and stops the works in two ways: every work at once, with "Pause all" and "Start all" in the page's bar, and each work, with its Start, Pause and Step in its row of the works list and in its window. Each work follows the global state unless the owner started it or paused it, and the later action wins, with one exception: Pause all stops every work, and makes the works the owner had started follow the global state again; Start all lets go on every work that follows it and lets new works start on ready items as slots free, while a work the owner paused, or the engine or the architect stopped, stays stopped; a work's Start lets it go on whatever the global state, its own pause lifted; a work's Pause stops it whatever the global state, and Start all does not lift it, which is the exception. Pauses take effect at the end of the round under way, which is never cut, because the engine's loop takes one step of one work at a time and reads between two steps what the owner asked. Step runs exactly one round of a work whatever the global state, and the work is paused by the owner after it, in the owner's hands until the owner starts it, its cell counting afresh as after any time the owner lets a work go on. A work that waits goes on only once what it waits for comes, and a work the owner starts that is paused, so holds no slot, goes on only once its machine has a slot free: the engine lifts its pause then. Instead of two modes of the engine as a whole, running and holding, in a panel behind the engine's state, and one work's round only while the engine held, because the owner did not find the panel, wants every work paused without ending any (as before a new flow version is installed), and wants each work started, paused or stepped on its own, to debug one work while the others stay paused; and instead of Start all lifting the works the owner paused, because the owner settled that a work's own pause holds until the owner starts that work.

Decision wait-holds-no-slot: A work that waits holds no slot, whether for the owner's or the architect's answer or at a row that waits (for other items, for the owner's ruling, for its turn to merge), so another work may start in the slot it left; once what it waits for came, it goes on only while its machine has a slot free, and before any new work starts on a ready item: the engine takes a step of each work that waits before it starts one. A move from one wait to another, as a question the architect hands to the owner or the owner hands back, takes no slot and is taken at once, and the owner's Step runs whatever the slots, as for a paused work.
