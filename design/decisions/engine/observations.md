# The observations

What agents come across beyond their works, kept for the owner as the installation's observations.

### decision observations-kept-with-the-finish
A run keeps, as it finishes, the observations of the return it was taken on and of each return of it sent back that no later return of it was made in a session holding; they are kept with the rest of the work and numbered in the same transaction. Their text, their cell and when they were kept are read from the work's records and are not copied.
Why: A later return made in the session that held one sent back says again what of it still holds, so keeping both would keep it twice; but a session begun anew after it (for another model or account, the owner, a flag, the run guard, a prompt too long) or begun again or compacted under its id holds only why it was sent back, and keeping only the return taken lost what the earlier one said. Saved with the finish, each is kept once whatever stops between (principle effects-known-as-done); the records hold the text already (principles state-from-records and stored-content-is-kept-small).

### decision observations-read-beside-the-engines-turn
The observations are read for the page, and the owner's reading of one is kept, through connections of their own to the store's file, not through the engine's one turn of changes to the works' records.
Why: Nothing decides by them and the tables of the readings are only added to, so a second writer cannot make a work's records diverge, as with the backup's records (decision backup-records-are-written-beside-the-engines-turn).

### decision the-planner-reads-observations-by-asking
Whoever plans the work reads the observations with their own command, the newest first, each time they are woken; `wait` says none of them.
Why: `wait` answers from a mark that is a place in the works' records (decision the-mark-is-a-place-in-the-records), and the observations are read through a connection of their own beside the engine's turn (decision observations-read-beside-the-engines-turn); a line for a new observation would need a second mark and a read of the store the rest of `wait` does not make. This rests on judgment, with no case measured.
