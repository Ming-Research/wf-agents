Decision: An agent's round records the message it was sent, word for word; each conditional input in it, with its address, the hash of its content and whether it was given or only referred to; and the call: the whole command line, the structured-output schema in it and not recorded again beside it, the session started or resumed, the level the round ran at, the engine's version (the commit it was built from) and the command line's version as it reported it when the engine started; the flow's descriptions of inputs are not recorded apart, because what an agent saw must be told afterwards without guessing, and the record of what each session was given is what the engine decides what the session holds from.

Decision: A round's record keeps each large text once, in `contents`, by the SHA-256 of the text: a conditional input given in the message, the cell's task, the structured-output schema of the command line; the record refers to each by its hash, and the message and the call are put back exactly, word for word, wherever they are read (the page, the owner's history), instead of each round holding every text it was given in full, because the shared formats, the rules, a cell's task and the schema are the same in round after round and session after session, and stored again each time they made most of the database.

Decision: The round of cell `start` records no input, because starting a work only takes the queue's next item and decides nothing; the engine's other steps record the values of their inputs by their sources, without the flow's descriptions.

Decision: Rounds recorded before this record keep their prompt and inputs as they were, and every reader (the engine, the interface's reads, the page) reads both, instead of rewriting old rounds, because a round is written once and never changed. A round recorded before its call named its level is read as at the one level the configuration at the round's commit maps its model and effort to, and as at no level when no one level does.

Decision: Rounds recorded before rounds were named by their work keep the addresses they were sent, a round's value named by R<number>, its cell and its path, which names no round now; what a session holds is still read from them (what it was given under any other address, and the values of its own returns, which its rounds' new names address), and an address of that form is never given again: a value a session was given only under one is given again under its R<work>-<n> address, instead of rewriting the old records into the new names, because a record is what was sent and is never changed, and giving such a value again costs a session its context once.
