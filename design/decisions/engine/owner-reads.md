# What the owner has read

What the engine keeps of what the owner read, and what it counts as new, changed or unread from it.

### decision the-engine-keeps-what-the-owner-read
The engine keeps in each work's records which versions of its blocks the owner read, and works out from them what is new, what changed and which replies are unread; no page keeps a record of its own.
- replaced: Drawing the marks of new and changed and the count of unread messages from made-up data alone, with nothing kept of what the owner read, was on main; the owner changed it, because he could not find a reply an agent had written to his words after he left the page.

### decision the-owners-first-reads-are-everything-but-the-replies
The engine began with every version of every block read except each version a run wrote to a cell's words block, which stays unread whether or not its work ended.
- rejected: an agent proposed counting every existing version as read and unread only from then on; the owner rejected it, because the old replies, the one he could not find among them, would not be marked, and he would still have to look for them by other means.
