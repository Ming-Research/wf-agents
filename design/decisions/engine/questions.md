# Questions and answers

What the engine keeps of the questions put to the owner and of the owner's answers, and what it writes of them.

### decision the-merge-gate-is-a-datum-the-engine-writes
Whether every question of the work that stands has the owner's answer to its latest version is the datum `asked`, a
choice the engine writes as a question is asked, asked anew or withdrawn and as the owner answers, where its value or
its words would change; before any question it is `answered` by its default. A cell waits for it as for any datum, by
its precondition: the check before the merge requires `answered`.
Why: A wait of the walk's own for the merge, which a cell would have named by a key of its own, was turned down: it
would be a kind of wait the flow's conditions cannot say and the page cannot show as it shows every precondition
(Article 7). Written only where it changes, it runs no cell.

### decision the-answers-are-the-owners-by-what-the-datum-declares
The datum `asked` declares that only the owner's acts change what its source writes (`changed_by = "owner"`), so that
the walk says the owner can make a precondition on it hold, and what waits on the owner gives, for that precondition,
each question that stands without the owner's answer. No reader of what a work waits for knows the datum by its name.
Why: Known by name, the page, the planner's list and the bar each held a copy of the rule, and a datum renamed or a
second one of its kind would have split them (principle one-thing-one-place).

### decision an-answer-runs-its-cell-by-the-engines-source
The block of the trigger an answer runs is written by the engine's source `answer` as the owner answers, never by the
owner nor by anything outside the work: the owner's act is the answer, and the engine works out from the question
whether it runs the cell. Neither that block nor `asked` is taken as written from outside.
Why: A block the owner wrote would run the cell for an answer that takes the option the work goes by, which statement
35 of the design document `data-and-context` says runs nothing, and would not stop a later round under way, as the
owner turning to a cell does; and a write of either from outside would say of the questions what their records do not.

### decision an-answer-to-an-earlier-version-is-kept
An answer to a version of a question that a later version replaced is kept in the owner's record, and answers nothing:
it runs no cell and leaves `asked` as it was. It is not refused.
Why: The owner answered what the page showed, which a round may have asked anew in the meantime; the record keeps what
the owner did (principle the-past-as-it-ran), and the question asked anew waits for an answer of its own.

### decision the-check-holds-a-protected-change-until-the-owner-lets-it-merge
The check before the merge looks at the work's own change under the paths the project protects, in the commit it
checks, against where that commit left main: each file by its path and the content it ends with. It does not pass while
that change is not empty and the owner has not let that very change merge, by the option of the engine's question that
says so, and it asks, or asks anew, where the question does not stand unanswered of that change; it is withdrawn once
the change is empty. A look the check cannot make fails it. As each round of a line that may change the work ends, the
engine looks too and asks early; a look that fails there asks nothing.
Why: Asked only as rounds ended, a change could merge unasked: a round stopped, a return unread, a run abandoned or a
look that failed left it unseen, and an answer to take the change back, no yes, let the merge go on. The check judges
the commit that merges, so nothing between the rounds and the merge escapes it. Told apart by content and not by the
text of the diff, the change asks nothing again as main moves on; asking at every round that touches the paths was
turned down, as the owner would answer the same change again and again.

### decision a-protected-change-is-asked-with-the-work-s-reason-for-each-file
The engine asks the owner of a change of the protected paths only once the work says why each file of it is needed: a return that does the cell's work, of a line that may change the work, is sent back for each file the branch changes as the round left it of which neither its `protected_changes` nor the question that stands says why, unless the owner let the file merge as it is. A reason stands for the file as it then is, another reason asks anew only while the question waits for its answer and never after the owner's yes to the same change, and the check before the merge, with no return to say why, asks with what the question that stands says.
Why: The owner judges a change of what governs the agents by why the agent made it, and a question with the diff alone
left that to guess (work 9). What the branch holds as the round ends is judged, not what that round's commits did, so
that a change left unexplained by a round stopped, failed or sent back is the next round's to explain; a yes the owner
gave without a reason, as to the check's question, settles it. Asking at once and adding the reason later was turned
down: the owner would be asked of the same change twice. The reason is kept in the question, which is where the owner
reads it and where a later round's look finds it. A new reason that took back a yes asked the owner again of a change
already let merge, only main having been merged into the branch since: the yes had been given to a question with no
reason, and the round that then gave one asked it anew (work 9).

### decision an-answer-to-the-engines-question-runs-the-last-cell-that-takes-one
The owner's answer to a question the engine asked is said to the last cell of the walk that has a trigger of an
answer, whichever cell's round it was asked with; the answer to an agent's question, to the asking cell; and the
owner's words about a question, to the cell its answer goes to. A cell that writes the work's questions and that no
answer runs, or no word of the owner's, is refused as its flow is compiled.
Why: The engine's question is of the work, not of what a round did: asked beside a round of the tester's or of the
check, its answer reached no cell that could act on it, and an answer to take a change back ran nothing. The last cell
that takes an answer is the one nearest the merge that changes the work, and the one to talk the question over with.
A cell that asks and takes no answer, or no words, would leave the owner's answer or remarks reaching no one, which
would show only once a work waited on it: words about a question of a cell no words ran were sent to the cell before it (work
4).

### decision the-questions-and-the-answers-are-one-record
Each version of a question, asked, asked anew or withdrawn, and the owner's answer to a question's latest version, is an
entry the engine writes, in the words of the record's own file, of the record the trigger of an answer of the cell the
answer goes to has its agent read (`q_and_a` in the engineer's flow), beside the records of questions and answers the
walk reads; every entry says all it needs to be read alone, and an answer to an earlier version is no entry. The record
runs no cell: the answer runs the asking cell by the block of its trigger.
Why: Kept in the engine's own records alone, the questions were never a version of the datum a cell was given, which so
read nothing (works 3 to 9); given apart from them, an answer could reach a cell without its question. Each cell that
takes the record is given it whole, as any datum by the kind its file names, and so a session reopened holds every
question with its answers; a second way of giving the answer, as the result of a call once was, was turned down
(design document `data-and-context`). Run by the record, the cells that wrote the plan ran again for their own
questions.

### decision a-return-asks-no-person-by-a-call
A return asks for tools alone, each a call that waits on nobody, and an agent asks the owner by the work's questions
(decision the-questions-and-the-answers-are-one-record); the engine's own call at a run's limit is the one call on the
owner. A question that one work's old records hold as a call on the owner is read as it was recorded: in the store, in
the work's history, and on its bar as a wait on the owner while it stood unanswered.
Why: A question in free words put by a call can be neither answered quickly nor told from a remark (principle
a-question-is-a-choice), and the past is read as it ran (principle the-past-as-it-ran). The call at a run's limit asks
for the run's let-go, which judges and recommends nothing and so is no question of the record (principle
a-question-is-a-choice), and which the engine records as a let-go of the cell, starting its counts anew (decision
the-same-sender-again).
