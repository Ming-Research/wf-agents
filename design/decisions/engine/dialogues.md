# Dialogues between cells

What the engine does with the dialogue a cell that makes something holds with each cell that judges or doubts it.

### decision the-owners-ruling-stands-until-the-judge-has-something-new
The owner settles a dispute by writing the judge's verdict in its place: the points written before it are new to no cell, and it stands until something the judge watches is new to it.
- rejected: an agent proposed the owner writing words to either side, which then did as told; the owner rejected it, because the owner's words are information and not orders, and the engine could not make sure of it.
- rejected: an agent proposed the judge running again on the owner's verdict, free to turn it back to a fail, its old points still waking the maker; the owner rejected it, because the ruling is to end the dispute.
- rejected: an agent proposed that a fail the owner writes in a verdict stand over the judge's later pass, by a block only he writes; the owner rejected it, because the verdict is the judge's, written by him only to end a dispute, and he stops a work by pausing it.
