# The accounts

The accounts the agents' program makes its calls under, which the owner adds on the page and switches between.

### decision an-account-is-a-name-and-a-token-in-the-keychain
An account is a name the owner gives and a token the agents' program makes to last a year (`claude setup-token`), kept in the macOS login keychain through `/usr/bin/security`, the token on its standard input, under a service keyed to the installation's id. The token is checked by the check of the outside before it is kept, and is in no file, setting, record, log, words of an error or answer of the page.
Why: The keychain keeps it encrypted at rest, where the program keeps the same kind of token; a file under `~/.wf-agents`, as GitHub's token is kept, was turned down for lying open on the disk, the Security framework called from the engine for trusting only the binary that made an item, which every install replaces (a judgment, not a trial), and a service keyed to the data directory's path for breaking when the directory moves and needing escaping in the line `security -i` reads.

### decision the-account-in-use-is-the-engines-own-state
Which accounts there are, and which is in use, are records of the store only added to (an account added, removed, taken), the engine's own state as whether sessions are kept warm is: the account in use is the latest taken and not removed since, or none. A token is kept before its account is recorded as added, and let go of after its account is recorded as removed, again at each removal and as the engine opens until it is.
Why: The page changes the account while the engine runs, and the engine reads the settings' file only as it starts, so an account kept there would wait for the next start; kept in this order, a recorded account never lacks its token, and a token left behind by a removal is let go of later (principle effects-known-as-done).

### decision the-account-is-handed-out-with-the-round
Every round is handed out with the account in use as it is handed out, none where none is; each try records that account's name, apart from the program's directory it ran with, and the program is given that same account's token, a round handed out before a switch running under the account it was handed out with. A token that cannot be given, as where the round was handed out with none, fails the round as a command line that cannot run, in the words of what keeps it or saying that no account is in use.
Why: Read again by the carrier as a try begins, the account recorded and the one whose token is given could part across a switch; and one field holding a directory or a name would have to be told apart by guessing.

### decision no-default-credential
The agents' program runs under no credential but the owner's accounts: a round and a keep-warm ping under the account in use, a fork under its session's account or the one in use (decision a-fork-runs-under-its-sessions-account), a look under each account added (decision usage-is-the-services-latest-word), and the check of a token under that token, before its account is added. With none in use the driver holds nothing back: a round handed out fails as a command line that cannot run, in the words beside the flows, its token taken before anything changes the work's branch, so that nothing is changed and nothing run; the wait every work shares carries it as it carries any command line that cannot run, and taking an account checks the outside at once and ends it. What a ping and the check do with none in use their own entries say (decision waiting-sessions-are-kept-warm, decision the-engine-checks-the-outside-itself). A script, which needs no account, runs as before. Neither the settings nor the program's own settings file may give the program another credential (decision the-carriers-own-variables-are-refused-in-agents-env, decision the-programs-directory-is-the-engines).
Why: The account is what the agents spend, and a fallback, as the sign-in of the program's directory, would spend one the owner did not take, unseen (principle nothing-passes-for-normal). A wait of its own for no account, and a gate in the driver, were turned down: none in use is a token that cannot be given, which the shared wait, its check, its words on the page and the check at once on a take already carry, and a second rule would say what the carrier finds.

### decision usage-is-the-services-latest-word
What the engine knows of an account's usage is what the agents' service last said of it, and when: the windows the latest rate limit event under the account named, by the names the service gives them, each with how much of it is used, when it resets and whether calls go through in it without a warning; or, where a call or a look met the account's limit and the service named no window, that the account is at its limit, with its reset where it was said. Every call says it under the account it was handed out with (a try, a keep-warm ping, a fork, a check of the outside, the check of a token as its account is added), never under the account in use as it is told; the page is told only where what is known changed, the same said again by each call of a round telling nothing. Each look that ends says from its own outcome whether it failed, and why: one that found the outside well, or the account at its limit, puts an earlier failure by. It is kept in the engine's memory and read by nothing that decides. As the owner opens the accounts the page asks for a look, which takes the check's short request under each account last heard of more than five minutes before, or never, all at once, with none in use as well; reading the accounts spends nothing.
Why: Under a token the program's own report of usage says nothing (`claude -p /usage` under a `setup-token` token prints only that the subscription is in use), so the event is the only source. Kept in the store it would be a record of the outside's word at a moment and not of what ran, which is why it was turned down; lost in a stop, it is heard again. Keyed by the account in use, a call handed out before a switch would put one account's usage under another. A look costs one request on the cheapest level's model, measured at $0.002, per account not heard of lately per opening, spent outside every work, and none while the page is not open; five minutes rests on judgment, usage moving slowly. Looks only with an account in use were turned down: the owner reads the usage to choose which to take.
