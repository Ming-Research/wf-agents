# The observations

The view of the observations.

### decision unread-is-a-dot-in-the-text-colour
An observation not yet read is marked by a dot in the text's colour, and one read is muted; the bar counts none of them.
Why: Orange says that something waits for the owner, which only a question that holds a work does (principle one-count-of-what-waits), and the accent says what is chosen; a mark in a colour of its own would be one more meaning to learn, a judgment with no case behind it.
