# The works

The list of works and the page of one work, whose history is drawn as the flow's matrix.

### decision the-list-is-ordered-by-a-column
The list of works shows #, Title, State, Cells, Level, Cell level, Cost, Time and Last, ordered by Last, the latest first, until the owner presses the head of another column.
- replaced: The columns Item, Title, Now, Cells, Taken and Last, ordered in five tiers of state the list did not show, was on main; the owner changed it, because the order looked random, Now was wide for a short word and Taken held four things.
- rejected: an agent proposed columns of fixed widths, ordered by #, Cost, Time or Last alone; the owner rejected it, because each column must widen by dragging its head, and a click on the head of each column that has an order must sort by it.
- rejected: the owner proposed the model's name beside each level; the owner rejected it, because level and model are two layers, another vendor's names are long, and an ended work would be named by today's mapping.

### decision taken-is-the-time-under-way
The time a work has taken is the time its rounds were under way, without its waits.
- rejected: an agent proposed counting a work's time from its start; the owner rejected it, because from its start the time does not show whether a work ran too long.

### decision a-works-page-shows-its-item-window
A work's page shows the console's item window for the work's item over its right, its Step showing the row of the history chosen; what the owner answers, chooses and writes is there alone.
- replaced: A work's page showing only its runs' records, apart from the item's window, was on main; the owner changed it, because from a work he could not look back at its item.
- rejected: an agent proposed the item's window at the left of the history; the owner rejected it, because the window of every other view opens on the right, and its tabs must be one with the console's.
- replaced: The work's usage shown and hidden by a click on the figures in the view's line was on main; the owner changed it, because it belongs in a tab of the window.
- replaced: A list of every block's versions, open by default, was on main; the owner changed it, because it is how the engine stores data, which meant nothing to him.
- replaced: A pinned line saying why the work stopped, and a box that wrote any block, was on main; the owner changed it, because the console says it where it happens, and each thing the owner writes has its own place.

### decision a-works-history-is-the-flows-matrix
A work's history is drawn as the flow's matrix: the cells across under the matrix's heads and the rows of its lines, one row a run, and the run's mark alone in its cell's column, outlined.
- replaced: A long list of the engine's records, one line each, was on main; the owner changed it, because the matrix's logic is the flow's, so the page should draw it alike.
- rejected: an agent proposed a box for each run in the matrix's columns with a short text after each row; the owner rejected it, because a cell full of words says less than the matrix's one mark.
- replaced: A history that showed no line, which cells share a session by, was on main; the owner changed it, because the lines should show plainly, drawn with the flow's own parts so that the two never drift apart.
- replaced: A light ground on the cell that ran, in place of its outline, was on main; the owner changed it, because on a dark page the ground could hardly be seen.

### decision a-row-is-a-run
A row of a work's history is a run of a cell, its left naming what woke it; a pause, a resume, a cancel or a reopening is a row half as high between runs, a dashed line in the accent.
- replaced: Rows also for the owner's acts that woke no cell, and for pauses and resumes, was on main; the owner changed it, because the left column then held two kinds of thing, where it should say what woke each run.
- rejected: an agent proposed the engine recording why a cell's turn came, to say more than first run; the owner rejected it, because a cell runs only when an input changed, which the row's latest input and the rows' order say.
- rejected: an agent proposed a thin rule between the runs for a pause; the owner rejected it, because a thin rule holds no words.
- rejected: an agent proposed no row for a pause, but a thick top edge on the run after it; the owner rejected it, because that would not show how long the work stood or who paused it.
- replaced: Pauses and resumes before a work's first run, drawn as any other, was on main; the owner changed it, because shown there they mean nothing.

### decision a-wake-is-an-arrow-from-the-writer
An arrow runs from the middle of the column of the cell whose run wrote what woke a run to that run's own column, under the cells, in the accent where it runs back.
- rejected: an agent proposed naming the datum at the left with the flow's signs of who writes, and marking in each cell what a run handed in and what it woke; the owner rejected it, because one arrow from the cell that wrote to the run says both.

### decision the-history-goes-under-the-window
What woke a run stands at its row's left, and how long it took and what it ran on at its right, where the window over the history may cover them while it is out.
- replaced: What each step did, every block it handed in, at the far right was on main; the owner changed it, because the window covered it there, and why a run ran matters more than when.
- replaced: When each run began, in the right column, was on main; the owner changed it, because how long each row took is what he wants to see there.

### decision the-model-a-round-recorded
A work's history names, at a run's right, the level, the reasoning effort and the model its latest round recorded it ran on, and none for a run of an engine cell.
- replaced: A history that named no level, effort or model was on main; the owner changed it, because each round records the model it ran on, which no later change of the settings makes untrue.
