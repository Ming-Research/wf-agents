# Dialogues between cells

What the engine does with the dialogue between a cell that makes something and each cell that judges or doubts it.

### decision the-owners-ruling-stands-until-the-judge-has-something-new
The owner settles a dispute by writing the judge's verdict in the judge's place: the points written before it are new to no cell, and it stands until something the judge watches is new to the judge.
- rejected: an agent proposed that the owner write words to either side, which would then do as told; the owner rejected it, because the owner's words are information and not orders, and the engine could not make sure of it.
- rejected: an agent proposed that the judge run again on the owner's verdict, free to turn it back to a fail, with its old points still waking the maker; the owner rejected it, because the ruling is meant to end the dispute.
- rejected: an agent proposed that a fail the owner writes in a verdict override the judge's later pass, through a block only he writes; the owner rejected it, because the verdict is the judge's, which he writes only to end a dispute, and he stops a work by pausing it.
