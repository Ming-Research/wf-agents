# Waiting

What a work waits on the owner for, and how whoever plans the work learns what needs them.

### decision nothing-is-pushed-to-the-planner
Whoever plans the work learns what needs them by `wf-agents wait`, a call that returns once something does; nothing is pushed to them.
- rejected: an agent proposed that the board push to whoever plans the work as each item is done; the owner rejected it, because a message pushed while the assistant talks with the owner breaks into that talk, whose context must stay on it.

### decision only-a-sign-in-waits-on-the-owner
Of the agents' command line failing, only what no try mends, as a sign-in refused or the program too old, waits on the owner, said with what to do; any other cause the engine waits out itself.
- rejected: an agent proposed counting every case of the agents' program not starting as waiting on the owner; the owner rejected it, because only a sign-in or a refusal does not mend without the owner.

### decision no-line-says-the-merge-waits
A question the owner is to answer is said by what it asks alone, and no line beside it says that the merge waits for it.
- rejected: an agent proposed the line "The merge waits for this answer." under a question's card; the owner rejected it, because the owner had called such a line needless, and the red card and the work's line at the check already say it.
