# What is said to a work

How the owner's words reach the cell the engine chooses, as a version of that cell's own words (design document
`data-and-context`, 35).

### decision words-go-only-to-a-cell-that-can-take-them
The owner's words go only to a cell with words of its own whose condition is met now, the engine choosing it and the page showing its choice before and after the words are sent; where there is none, or the work has ended, they are refused with why and go to no other cell. A line none of whose cells has words of its own is not counted as standing anywhere.
Why: The page chose the cell itself and, finding none for the cell that asked, wrote the words for the cell before it without a word (work 4); words written for a cell that does not apply would wait unseen (principle nothing-passes-for-normal). Counted as where the line stands, the reading of the tree a reset or compacted session begins with, which no words run, left the line taking none until a later cell ran again.

### decision words-to-a-round-count-once-its-session-held-them
Words told to a round under way count as taken by it only once its program has ended, and only those its session is found to have been given; the rest stay new to the cell, which runs again for them. So too for a run abandoned meanwhile: its return is dropped, but what its session was given counts.
Why: Counted as taken when said, words reached a round whose agent used no tool again and were consumed unseen, and its session was given them again with the next message, since nothing said it held them; the same where the round was then stopped.
