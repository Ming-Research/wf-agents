# The observations

What agents come across beyond their works, kept for the owner as the installation's observations.

### decision an-observation-is-a-plain-text
An observation is a plain text of what an agent came across beyond its work and where, kept as the installation's with its work, cell and round, and given to no cell.
- rejected: an agent proposed that each observation say whether it does harm now; the owner rejected it, because only a defect can do harm.
- rejected: an agent proposed that each observation say what follows if it is left; the owner rejected it, because that was strange as well, and how urgent each is the architect judges.
- rejected: an agent proposed giving a work's first cell the observations its item took in; the owner rejected it, because observations are the architect's, written, kept, deleted and merged by it, as items are.
