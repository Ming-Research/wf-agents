# The item's window

What the console opens for an item: its background, its problem and its plan, the questions in them and the
discussion.

### decision the-owner-writes-to-a-line
The discussion offers each line of the work's flow that has a cell the owner speaks to, and names on it the cell the
words reach, the one of that line that ran last; a line none of whose cells has run cannot be chosen, and unless the
owner picks another the line that ran last is chosen. What is sent is said to the line.
Why: The owner speaks to each line of a work apart, the engineer, the deep researcher and the reviewers; a fixed list of
three cells of one flow left the reviewers out.

### decision an-option-is-picked-by-pressing-it
An option of a question is a button: pressing it picks it, and pressing the one picked lets it go.
Why: A row of short labels under the options, each to be matched to its option, was turned down as easy to press
wrongly, and radio buttons under the options as saying each option twice.
