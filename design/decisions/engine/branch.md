# What the engine reads of a work's branch

### decision the-branch-is-read-in-one-step
The data of a work's branch, its design tree and the work's head, are read in one step, at the branch and not at what its worktree has checked out: in the round that takes main in for a run, as each round of the work ends, by the check as it judges and by the merge before it merges. A version is written only where what is read differs from the latest, and each datum says in one place what makes one.
Why: Two readings would drift apart in when they read and in what they count as new. A run's later rounds read nothing as main is taken in, since what they would find is what the run's earlier rounds committed.

### decision a-version-read-at-a-rounds-end-is-that-rounds
What is read as an agent's round on a line that may change the work ends is what the round committed: its return hands it in, and its run writes it as it finishes. Anything else is no round's and is written from outside: what is read as main is taken in, put into that round; what the end of a round of a line that only reads finds; what an act reads. A round that failed, or whose return was sent back, hands in nothing, and what it committed is read at the end of the round that takes it up.
Why: Written from outside, a tester's commit would be an outside reason for the review to run, which sets the review's bound on going round back to nought, and a round's own change of the design tree would be told to its session as new. Written from outside as a round fails, a commit would run the review on half-done work before the round is taken up; waiting loses nothing, the check reading the head in any case. That a person's commit made while a round runs is taken as the round's rests on judgment: nothing tells the two apart.

### decision a-read-is-no-newer-than-a-version-the-round-never-knew
What a round's carrier read, of the work's branch or of main, written for the round or from outside, becomes its block's latest version only where the block's latest is one the round knew: written before the round began, of the round's own reading, or told to it. Over a version written since that the round never knew, nothing is written, the read being taken as no newer: of a read for the round, the latest version that says what it read is put in, where one does, as what the round took in. Each version written from what a round read says the round's run.
Why: A read and its write reach the loop each its own way: main a check read before main moved, told to its round after the watch had written the newer head, was written over it, and main went back to the older head, which the check and the review then acted on (as the player of scripts met it). Telling reads apart by when they were read lost: every carrier would have to stamp its reads by a clock the loop trusts, and a test's paused clock gives many reads one time; whether the round knew a version is in the records alone. Kept for what changed under a round, nothing is lost: what it never knew is new to its cell, which runs on it again.
