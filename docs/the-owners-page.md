# What the owner's page is still to hold

The parts of the owner's page that are designed and not built, or built only in part: the views that say nothing yet,
and what the built views lack. Each statement is what the page is to do, with its reason, and where a way was turned
down, which and why. The work that builds the rest of a part takes it out of here, and what of it then needs saying is
a decision of the view it was built in.

The page's principles hold for everything here and are not said again: Article 8, the principles of the owner's page
and those of its look.

## The flow's view

1. In a cell's panel, the rules an agent works under, which every agent is given alike, are shown among the cell's
   inputs, as one kind of input, and not as a part of its own: an input is of several kinds.

## A work's page

2. A round's panel shows first what was new to the round, apart from what its session already held, and then all that
   was sent, as it was sent, with nothing left out. One list of a round's inputs, the sent ones bold and the held ones
   grey, was turned down: it did not show what the agent got this round.

## The item's window

3. What an item depends on can be changed on the page, while its work has started no run (decision
   dependencies-until-the-work-starts): the owner needs no command line for it (principle
   the-console-is-where-the-owner-works).

4. The owner's words can carry pictures, picked or pasted, sent with the words as one message and shown with them,
   and shown in the rounds that were given them: what the owner sees of a problem can be shown to the agent as it is.

5. Pictures waiting to be sent stand as small pictures above the text, each removable, with how many there are and how
   many there may be; what the box refuses is said file by file. A place for files of the page's own beside the box,
   and sending pictures apart from the words, were turned down: small pictures with a cross are how the chat programs
   the owner uses show what waits.

6. A picture the owner sent is shown small and opens in a new tab, the simplest way to see a picture whole and at its
   own size, which the browser can also scale and save; opening it in a dialog was turned down.

## The lists

7. The buttons that filter a list by state are signs without words: the words for the states are too long for one
   row.

8. A list remembers in the browser which states the owner hid, not which are shown, so that a state the page comes to
   have later is shown until the owner hides it.

9. The buttons of states are one part for every list, and one list has one choice for all that is in it, kept in the
   browser. Where the owner narrows a list by picking, a button picked is drawn even when nothing matches it, so
   that the choice can be taken back.

## The observations

10. The observations are narrowed by picking tags and by picking those still to handle or those handled: with nothing
    picked all are shown; a row is shown when it matches any of what is picked within a group, and does so in each
    group; clearing takes every choice back. There will be many, each with the project's tags, and the owner starts
    from all of them and picks a few. Listing every tag at first with the handled ones hidden, and picking one tag
    only beside a search, were turned down.

11. A row of an observation begins with its title, with its content under it cut to a few lines, and a line between
    rows. One recorded with no title keeps the start of its words and is given no title it was not recorded with. With
    a row cut to one line of its first words the owner had to open each to know what it was.

## The system

12. The view of the system shows how the whole system stands, in parts: what goes on now, what was produced, what was
    spent, what was wasted, and how well it runs. The owner wants every side of it, not the spending alone.

13. The view is all of the measures, each line of one watched by a monitor of its own, the simplest the mean and the
    spread of seven days, and a spike raises a warning. There is no part for what is out of the ordinary: the watching
    is on the line itself, and a page where everything takes long to find is of no use.

14. The lines of every chart of numbers take their colours in one order of hues (Article 8).

## The settings

15. Each part of the settings shows first what the page can change and then what only the file can; of the project's
    constitution only its path is written, since its words are the project's own. The owner governs the whole
    configuration in one place and must see at a glance which is which. The accounts the agents run under stand with
    what the page changes, though they are the engine's own state and no key of the file: they change at once, where
    the rest of what the page changes is saved. So does the switch that lets the engine move to another account at a usage limit,
    above the accounts, with the engine's latest moves listed below them; it acts when pressed, as the accounts do. A
    page of its own for the levels alone was turned down.

16. In a form written as a whole by one saving, nothing takes effect before it is saved, so what can be on or off
    there is a box to tick and not a switch that acts when pressed; and a list the owner edits row by row is a table.
