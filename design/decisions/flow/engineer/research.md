# The research

How a work comes to its plan: the reading of the design tree (R0), the research (R1), the deep research (D1), and the look for a better way (R2, D2).

### decision the-tree-is-read-in-a-cell-of-its-own
R0 is there so that the design tree is read before any work on the item: it understands the item only as far as it must to know where in the tree to look, and hands in nothing.
- replaced: R0 on a line of its own, gathering the history into a brief of at most 500 words for R1 to read in a new session, was on main; the owner changed it, because R0 exists since agents do not read the tree, or not deeply, and its reading stays in the session R1 goes on in.
- replaced: R0 handing in the decisions related to the item and a supplement to its background was on main; the owner changed it, because their first reason, what a reopened session would lack, was gone once a new session has R0 read the tree again.
- rejected: an agent proposed that R0 also confirm the defect or the need in the code and its history; the owner rejected it, because R0's one purpose is that the tree is read, agents having skipped it and changed the code at will countless times.
- rejected: an agent proposed putting the whole design tree into the line's session in place of R0's reading; the owner rejected it, because the tree of a larger project would overflow it at once.

### decision the-research-asks-of-no-other-item-unbidden
R0's and R1's tasks send them to look at no other item and at no earlier work on the item; `query`, which every agent cell may ask for, is there where the item needs it.
- rejected: an agent proposed that R0 ask `query` for the items around its own and the item's earlier works, or that the engine give it them as data; the owner rejected it, because most items need only themselves, and the tool is for when it is needed.
- replaced: R1 opening with an order to ask `query` whether the item was worked on before was on main; the owner changed it, because every research's first round went on a query though most items were never worked on.

### decision the-research-may-make-what-its-problem-needs
R1 and D1 may make code, data and tests for what they research: a test goes with the project's tests, and anything else into `project.research`, committed.
- replaced: R1 handing in only a report and changing only `research/`, held by a check, was on main; the owner changed it, because the limit is there to keep the research on its problem, not to guard against the agent.
- rejected: an agent proposed bringing back the old research's rules for `research/`, such as deleting empty probes; the owner rejected it, because the engineering rules already say whom a file serves and when it is removed.

### decision deep-research-only-for-a-fact-or-a-trial
R1 says `deep_needed` yes only where the direction rests on a fact it cannot check in a round or two, or on trying several ways first; a choice for the owner is a question.
- replaced: Every work going through research and then a design stage was on main; the owner changed it, because the research concludes simple things itself, and only very complex ones need the deep research.
- replaced: The deep research entered on any approval, open choice or contradiction was on main; the owner changed it, because the deep research is taken only where deep research is needed: of ten works that entered it in one night, six should not have.

### decision the-way-forks-before-the-research
Whether the deep research is needed is R1's `deep_needed`, a condition of R1 and D1 alike: R1 runs while it is no, D1 once it is yes, and the one running answers the plan's dialogues.
- replaced: R1 and D1 one after the other, D1 entered after R1, was on main; the owner changed it, because the fork R1 decides belongs before it, so that a later pass goes from R0 to R1 or to D1, with no two sets of blocks.
- rejected: an agent proposed that the plan's judges write their points into one block for the research and one for the deep research, by which held the plan; the owner rejected it, because nothing stops them writing into the wrong one.
- rejected: an agent proposed leaving the design's paragraph on a maker's dialogues as it was, naming no maker where two cells may write the plan; the owner rejected it, because that paragraph did not hold where two cells may write the plan.

### decision the-research-hands-over-with-what-it-found-alone
With `deep_needed` yes, R1's return holds only `found`: no plan, no question and no answer, as `deep_needed` declares (`with_nothing_but`), and one that holds more is sent back saying what.
- replaced: R1 told to write only `found` with yes, which the engine did not check, was on main; the owner changed it, because a question R1 handed in while deciding deep had no cell left for the owner to speak to of it, and the deep research excludes every other output.
- rejected: an agent proposed that a research sending the work to the deep research still hand in its plan and questions; the owner rejected it, because a research that cannot decide a design writes no draft of one, and its questions are set in the draft.
- rejected: an agent proposed that one return carry the answer to the owner beside `found`; the owner rejected it, because the rule has the cell answer the owner first, and decide the deep research in a later return.

### decision the-better-way-is-looked-for-once
R2 and D2 look for a better way once a work, until a run of theirs has handed in (`{cell}_handed_in`), and hand it to the plan's writer in `better_found`, a few sentences, or nothing.
- replaced: R2 and D2 run on each change of the plan, writing the plan themselves, asking and spoken to, was on main; the owner changed it, because later changes of a plan are small, and in one work R2 ran four times and handed in nothing.
- rejected: an agent proposed that R2 watch `deep_needed`, whose first version runs a cell once; the owner rejected it, because the cell would then be run by what it does not read.
- rejected: an agent proposed asking whether there is a better way in the plan's review, on another line; the owner rejected it, because asked again in the writer's own session a better way is often found: in the old flow, 40 times in 52.
- rejected: the owner proposed that `better_found` only mark that a better way was found, its writer knowing the rest from the session; the owner rejected it, because the way is written in case the session was replaced and for the owner to see, though no longer than needed.
- rejected: an agent proposed accepting that no one looks for a better way again once a plan changes greatly; the owner rejected it, because a plan changed that much is a new item, the old one cancelled.

### decision the-better-way-is-asked-by-two-cells-with-one-task
R2 and D2 are two cells, one on each line that writes the plan, given one task written once in `tasks/better_way.md`.
- rejected: an agent proposed one cell running on the line of whichever cell wrote the plan; the owner rejected it, because a cell is on one line, and the question must be asked in the session of the plan's writer.
