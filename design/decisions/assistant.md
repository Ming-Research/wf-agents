# The owner's assistant

The session beside the owner that thinks designs through with him, and looks at the system and acts on it for him.

### decision the-assistant-works-outside-the-engine
The owner's assistant is a session outside the engine that looks at the system and acts on it through the program's commands; no part of it runs in the engine.
- rejected: an agent proposed running the assistant inside the system, with its conversation kept in the database; the owner rejected it, because inside the system it would die with the system, grow into a second architect with a queue, and hold the highest rights while running unattended.

### decision the-assistant-has-no-switch
Nothing in the system limits what the assistant does for the owner: it works with his permissions, and no switch sets how far it may confirm things in his place.
- rejected: an agent proposed a mechanical switch setting how far the assistant may confirm things in the owner's place; the owner rejected it, because the assistant has full permission and does what he tells it.
